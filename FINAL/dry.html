Dry skin, also known as xerosis, occurs when your skin loses moisture and hydration, leading to rough, flaky, or scaly patches. Common causes include cold or dry weather, sun damage, harsh soaps, and overbathing. To relieve dry skin, dermatologists recommend moisturizing regularly, using gentle cleansers, and protecting your skin from the sun. Additionally, it's important to stay hydrated and consider using a humidifier in dry environments. For persistent issues, consulting a dermatologist may be beneficial.
