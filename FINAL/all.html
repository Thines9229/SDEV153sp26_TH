Understanding your skin type is essential for selecting the right skincare routine. While genetics largely determine skin type, factors like age, environment, diet, stress, and sun exposure can influence it over time. Proper care involves using products suited to your skin’s moisture, oil balance, and sensitivity, while also considering acne or aging concerns and sun protection based on Fitzpatrick type
